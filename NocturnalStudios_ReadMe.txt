OVERVIEW

To pay off your college debts, you have chosen to work in Luminara Zoo as a nightshift zookeeper. 
Take care of the animals, earn your wages, and make it through to 6 am. 
Do well and you may unveil the secrets of the zoo...
Make sure not to let the animals escape! 

=============================================================================
CONTROLS


Scene switching using number keys in the game

- Press **1** to load Scene 1 (Horse)
- Press **2** to load Scene 2 (Flamingo)
- Press **3** to load Scene 3 (Seal)
- Press **4** to load Scene 4 (Owl)

Point & Click to interact with objects on the screen
